MemeScout Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

MemeScout may process information necessary to operate its Discord bot, including Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, command usage, watchlists, and bot configuration settings.

2. Blockchain Information

If MemeScout provides blockchain monitoring or trading-related features, publicly available blockchain information may be processed, including token addresses, transaction data, wallet addresses, balances, and transaction activity.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide MemeScout features, respond to commands, send alerts, maintain watchlists, improve the service, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services that are necessary to operate MemeScout, such as Discord, blockchain data providers, hosting providers, or other infrastructure providers.

5. Wallet Security

MemeScout will not ask users to provide their cryptocurrency recovery phrase or private key through Discord. Users are responsible for protecting their own wallet credentials.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve MemeScout, or as required by applicable law.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of MemeScout by contacting the MemeScout operator through the official MemeScout Discord server.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by MemeScout. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

MemeScout is not intended for users who are not permitted to use Discord or cryptocurrency-related services under applicable laws and platform rules.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the MemeScout operator through the official MemeScout Discord server.
